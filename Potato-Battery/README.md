
# 🥔 Potato Battery – Electricity from a Potato

An interactive electronics experiment demonstrating the principle of a potato battery. The project explores how an electrochemical reaction between different electrodes can generate electrical potential and how this energy interacts with an electronic circuit. Designed and simulated in Autodesk Tinkercad Circuits.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Potato Battery project demonstrates the conversion of chemical energy into electrical energy using a potato as an electrolyte.

Different electronic components are connected to the experimental circuit to explore electrical behavior and the principles of electrochemical energy generation.

## ⚙️ How it Works (System Logic)

1. **Electrochemical Cell**
   - Two different metal electrodes are inserted into the potato.
   - The potato acts as an electrolyte.
   - An electrochemical reaction creates a potential difference between the electrodes.

2. **Electrical Circuit**
   - The potato battery is connected to the electronic circuit.
   - DIP switches allow different circuit configurations.
   - Potentiometers provide adjustable resistance.

3. **Output Components**
   - LEDs provide visual indication.
   - A DC motor demonstrates electrical energy conversion into mechanical motion.
   - A light bulb demonstrates electrical energy conversion into light.

4. **Experimentation**
   - Different component configurations can be explored.
   - The circuit demonstrates the relationship between chemical energy and electrical energy.

## 🚀 Installation & Use

1. Open the project in Autodesk Tinkercad Circuits.
2. Examine the potato battery and electrode connections.
3. Start the circuit simulation.
4. Adjust the potentiometers.
5. Change the DIP switch configurations.
6. Observe the behavior of the connected electronic components.
